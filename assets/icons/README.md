# Travel icons

`truck-camper.svg`: dark pickup with cream/turquoise travel trailer, based on your photo. Suggested map size: 80 × 40 px.

`plane.svg`: matching airplane, nose pointing up. Suggested map size: 36 × 36 px.

Both have transparent backgrounds and white outlines for visibility. SVG source is editable. These assets are not yet assigned to route points; that requires transport-mode data.

`silver-car.svg`: small silver car used during ground-travel playback in Nicaragua, Japan, and Hawaii (54 × 27 px). Other ground travel uses the camper.

`bullet-train.svg`: simplified side-profile adaptation of
[Shinkansen head illustration side view](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Shinkansen_head_illustration_side_view.png)
by [Gabrielchl](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/User:Gabrielchl), licensed
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Redrawn as SVG,
matched to the reference’s white body, dark blue stripe, black windshield,
and gray skirt, and simplified for small map markers.
