About

A little about us.

I'm Ryan Hollis, a backend and distributed systems engineer from Mississippi, living in Atlanta. My wife, Cristhiam Torrez, is from Nicaragua.

We married on April 26, 2025. After the wedding, we took an extended honeymoon with our dog, Bento, visiting 40 national parks in our camper. We also traveled to Hawaii, Japan, and Nicaragua.

Our home on the road

We renovated our camper and equipped it with solar panels and an off-grid electrical system. That let us spend more time away from hookups, with the flexibility to stay close to the places we wanted to explore.

We often chose dispersed camping in national forests near the national parks we visited. The camper was our base for hiking, exploring, and everyday life between stops—with Bento along for the journey.
